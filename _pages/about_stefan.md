### Stefan Liu

Stefan received dual B.S. degrees in Biochemistry and Neurobiology from the University of California, San Diego in 2022. He worked as a research technician in the Gupta Lab at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where he focused on mouse colony management, organoid models, and drug resistance projects. His research interests center on the tumor microenvironment, particularly the crosstalk between early immune responses and metastatic potential. Outside the lab, Stefan enjoys film photography and skiing with his family.
